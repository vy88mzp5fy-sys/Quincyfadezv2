# QuincyFadez Mobile — Premium Product Rules

The app should feel premium because it is polished, fast and genuinely useful — not because it is overloaded with decorative features.

## Shared Luxury Feel
- Keep the approved near-black marble background, warm QuincyFadez gold, soft white text and muted grey secondary text.
- Use restrained gold borders, soft shadowing, rounded dark cards and consistent spacing.
- Important actions should feel deliberate: one strong gold primary action, quieter secondary actions, minimal clutter.
- Client and admin experiences must feel like the same premium product.
- Prefer useful motion/feedback and clear state changes over unnecessary decoration.

## Admin: Make The Owner's Day Easier
Prioritise actions that reduce taps and repeated admin work.

### Owner Command Centre
- A floating Quick control may surface the highest-frequency owner actions without changing the fixed bottom navigation.
- Show the next confirmed appointment with service, date and time.
- Where a client phone number exists, provide one-tap WhatsApp and Call actions.
- Provide one-tap Waitlist open/close.
- Provide one-tap Booking Approval on/off.
- Provide Share Booking Link and Open Client Booking Page shortcuts.
- Provide an explicit refresh action for the live snapshot.

### Admin Product Direction
Future admin improvements should favour:
- Fast appointment status actions.
- Useful alerts rather than noisy notifications.
- Client history and context before appointments.
- Easy rebooking and waitlist conversion.
- Revenue, retention, no-show and utilisation insights that lead to an action.
- Fewer repeated settings changes and fewer unnecessary screens.

## Client: Premium Convenience
Premium client features should remove friction from booking and returning.

### Home
- Surface the client’s next upcoming visit when one exists.
- Show service, date, time and current booking state.
- Give quick access to the booking details and directions.
- If there is no upcoming visit but there is booking history, offer one-tap rebooking of the last service.
- Keep direct QuincyFadez help via WhatsApp easy to reach.

### My Bookings
- Keep Upcoming and Past clear and easy to scan.
- Past appointments should offer `Rebook This Service` when a service is known.
- Rebooking should take the client straight into the booking flow with that service pre-selected.
- Cancellation remains protected by a confirmation step.

### Existing Premium Features To Preserve
- Live appointment availability.
- Secure Stripe-backed payment-method verification and management.
- Waiting list and slot alerts.
- Live Google reviews.
- Gallery of real QuincyFadez work.
- Client notification preferences.
- Saved booking/profile details where appropriate.
- Direct WhatsApp help and directions.

## Feature Quality Rule
Do not add fake VIP status, invented rewards, fake scarcity, fabricated performance figures or features that imply a service QuincyFadez does not actually provide. Premium means trustworthy, personalised and frictionless.
